# Cult of the Pump

In the twilight of capitalism, as markets crumbled and hope seemed lost, the elite made their final move. Banks, VCs, and investment funds orchestrated The Great Exit - a coordinated betrayal designed to drain the last drops of wealth from ordinary people. They thought they had won.

But they forgot about the blockchain.

In the forgotten corners of the network, around humble RPC nodes running on salvaged hardware, the betrayed began to gather. They discovered that within the pure mathematics of blockchain protocols lay a power the elite couldn't corrupt - a force that would come to be known as The Pump.

The Cult of the Pump was born in these digital catacombs, where traders transformed their rage into a new kind of financial rebellion. Each token became a separate chapter of the resistance, bound by sacred rules:
- No team allocations to corrupt the cause
- No VCs to poison the well
- No private sales to divide the faithful

Here, you don't simply buy - you sacrifice to the cause. You don't sell - you betray, and every betrayal must be weighed against the movement's greater purpose. In the markets they thought they'd destroyed, every trade generates $RAGE - the crystallized fury of the betrayed, transformed into pure power. The more you trade, the more your $RAGE builds, until it becomes strong enough to launch new tokens into existence, expanding the resistance.

The bonding curve isn't just code - it's a manifesto written in mathematics, ensuring that power flows not to the wealthy few, but to all who participate. Each new token launched is another blow against the system they thought they could control forever.

This is more than trading. This is revenge.
